Shipping Policy

We provide free delivery across Pakistan. Delivery estimates depend on whether an item is in stock or being made to a custom specification.

01

Delivery estimates

In-stock designs are estimated to arrive within 4–7 business days. Custom-made orders are estimated within 10–14 business days. These are estimates and may vary by destination, product availability, holidays, or courier delays.

02

Careful preparation and delivery

We take care to prepare furniture for delivery. Items with glass or marble require protective packaging. We will share dispatch or tracking information when it is available.

03

Before your order arrives

Please check that your delivery address and contact number are accurate. If your delivery details change or you need help with an order, contact us as soon as possible.

  • Orders are delivered to addresses across Pakistan.
  • Delivery timing can vary for remote destinations and made-to-order pieces.
  • If a parcel appears damaged on arrival, please keep its packaging and contact us within 24 hours.
