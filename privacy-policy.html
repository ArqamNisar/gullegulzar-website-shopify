This notice explains how Gull e Gulzar Home Trolley uses information you share when browsing our store, placing an order, or contacting our team.

01

Information you provide

Depending on how you use the store, information may include your name, phone number, email address, delivery details, order information, and messages you send to our support team.

02

How information is used

We use information relevant to your request to manage orders, arrange delivery, respond to questions, provide customer support, and maintain the operation and security of the store.

03

Service providers and retention

Information needed to fulfil an order may be shared with service providers involved in store operations, payment processing, or delivery. We retain information as needed to provide our services and meet applicable record-keeping obligations.

04

Your questions and choices

To ask about personal information associated with an order or support request, contact us using the details on our Contact Us page. Shopify may also process information when you browse or use the store; its privacy practices are described in Shopify’s own privacy materials.

For privacy questions, email gullegulzarhometrolley@gmail.com or contact our team.
