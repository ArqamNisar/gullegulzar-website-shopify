Returns & Refund Policy

If something isn’t right with your delivery, contact our team within 24 hours.

Last updated October 2026Questions? Contact our team →

We want your furniture to arrive in the condition you expected. If an order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact us so we can review it and arrange an appropriate resolution.

01

Report an issue within 24 hours

Please contact us within 24 hours of delivery if an item is damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered. Include your order details and clear photos or a short video of the item and its packaging so our team can assess the issue.

02

Review and resolution

After reviewing a verified issue, our standard resolution is to repair the affected item or provide a replacement. Please wait for our team to confirm the next steps before sending an item back.

  • Keep the original packaging until we have reviewed your report.
  • Share your order number and contact details when you reach out.
  • Do not arrange a return shipment without speaking to us first.
03

Custom orders and cancellations

Custom orders cannot be cancelled once fabrication has begun. Please contact us as early as possible if you need to discuss a change before production starts.

04

Refund questions

Refunds are not automatic. If a repair or replacement is not suitable for a verified issue, contact us to discuss the available resolution. We will confirm any approved refund and its method with you before it is processed.

To report an issue, contact us or message our team on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery.
