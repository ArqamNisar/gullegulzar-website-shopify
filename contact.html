A few quick answers

Before you get in touch

How long does delivery take?

In-stock designs are estimated within 4–7 business days; custom-made orders within 10–14 business days. See our Shipping Policy.

What if my furniture arrives damaged?

Contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order details and clear photos or a short video so we can review it. See our Returns & Refund Policy.

Can I cancel a custom order?

Custom orders cannot be cancelled once fabrication has begun. Contact us as early as possible if you need to discuss a change.
